About

Sound with a purpose.

Arthur Taine is an independent music project focused on Deep House, Jazz House and electronic music. Blending old-school influences with modern production, the project explores atmosphere, rhythm and uplifting energy. Arthur Taine composes and produces original music, writes the lyrics and shapes each arrangement, using AI-generated vocals as a creative tool.
